How the price of a Kushaq chassis part is forecast

The part is Chassis A39, KUS-CHS-00312, supplied by Bharat Forge. It is steel, bought from a domestic vendor, and repriced by the vendor every six months. This page follows the order in which you would open it on the dashboard.

First, a note on July 2023

The data starts in July 2023, so that month is the first actual price (₹1,592.98), not a forecast. The dashboard calls it the Nomination or Budget (BG) price. No model ever predicted it. Every forecast on screen looks forward from June 2026, the last month of history: July 2026 is the next-month forecast and December 2026 is the six-month forecast.

Say thisJuly 2023 is where this part's story starts: the price SKODA budgeted when the part was nominated. The model's job is to say where it goes from here, and the dashboard shows how far it has already moved from that budget.

What decides this price

The model works from a simple idea: a part's price is its starting level, moved by a handful of outside forces, each with its own delay. For this chassis:

The monthly retrain step ranks these clues and keeps the ones that earn their place, always retaining price history, currency, matched commodity and freight. Two seasoned models run side by side, XGBoost (machine learning) and SARIMA (classic statistics), and a seasonal baseline acts as the yardstick.

Say thisFor next month the model sees no strong push either way. The price is already well above its long-run average, which pulls it back, and that is offset by small lifts from peers, freight and geopolitics. Over six months, the long-run tendency of Bharat Forge chassis parts to rise, plus geopolitical risk and the rupee, outweighs that pull-back.
Watch outThis explanation comes from re-running the same XGBoost setup on the same data. It lands close to the dashboard's forecast but is not identical (₹2,096 vs ₹2,115 for July, ₹2,201 vs ₹2,189 for December), so quote the drivers, not the exact rupee figure. A small push is not a prediction: on the Hierarchy tab, most parts are flagged low confidence for exactly this reason.
